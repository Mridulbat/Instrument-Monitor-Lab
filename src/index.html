How to read this experiment

The demo includes high and low process excursions plus missing, frozen, and out-of-range signals. Calibration maps the example transmitter to 0–100 units. Invalid values are visible for diagnosis but are excluded from reference RMSE. A steady real process can also trigger STUCK: this rule is advisory. Active alarms are held during bad quality; two valid samples confirm recovery. Drift checks use a reference when available.
